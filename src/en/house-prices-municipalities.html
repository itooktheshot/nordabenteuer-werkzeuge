How to read the average. It is the mean of all purchases in a year, not the median — a single manor house visibly lifts a small municipality. Where the sales column shows a low number, the average is correspondingly shaky — below roughly fifteen sales a year I would read it as a direction, not as a price. That is why the sales column stays visible on a phone and the change column gives way instead.

The figure is the köpeskilling, the purchase price itself. What comes on top — stamp duty, mortgage deeds, the registration fee — is worked out by the buying costs page. Running costs depend on the house far more than on the municipality.
