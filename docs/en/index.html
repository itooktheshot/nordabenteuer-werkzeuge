How these pages work Everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and no other website is contacted — no property portal either. You paste text in, the page reads it, done. Each tool uses figures that change every year (stamp duty, preschool fees, patient fees). The date is shown at the bottom of each page. If a number is out of date: let me know.

These tools are part of nordabenteuer.de, a guide to moving to Sweden and buying a home there. The explanations come from the articles; where a tool stops, consulting takes over.
