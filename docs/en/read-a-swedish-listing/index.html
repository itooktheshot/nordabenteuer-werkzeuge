Read a Swedish property listing

Copy the fact list from a Swedish property listing and paste it here. Every line gets explained in English, and the numbers the listing leaves out get calculated for you. Works with the Swedish original, the English Hemnet app and browser-translated pages. Nothing leaves your browser.

The block with Utgångspris, Avgift, Boarea and so on is enough — or Asking price, Monthly fee, Living area if your page is in English. Order and line breaks don't matter.

This page calculates in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and no other website is contacted.

What the listing doesn't say

    What this page can't do: tell you whether the fee is high for the area, what shape the housing association is in, or where the final price will land. That takes comparable sales and a look at the annual report (årsredovisning).

    If that's what you need: Property check, 149 € — two pages in English, usually within two working days.
